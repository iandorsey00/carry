# Notice the clue

Start with the setting. A riding clue points to a domestic animal. A pond clue may point to an amphibian, an animal that often spends part of its life in water and part on land.

If several names come to mind, notice the action or sound in the clue before choosing one.

The practice cues are adapted from the example study set in [memtrain](https://github.com/iandorsey00/memtrain/blob/main/animals.csv), available under the MIT license.
